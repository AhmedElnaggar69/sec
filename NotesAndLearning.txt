confidentiallty - passive attacks , hacker can hear the audio but not alter it

integrity - active attacks , hacker can change the audio

authenticity - the audio came from the intended sender to the receiver

freshness - new audio not resent or old


Hybrid design: RSA is slow and can only encrypt small data, while AES is fast. So RSA delivers a small AES key at the start, and AES then handles the heavy audio. This is the architecture we're building.
